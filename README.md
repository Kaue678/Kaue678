👋 About me

My research at UFOPA, Brazil, connects artificial intelligence, data science, structural bioinformatics and molecular bioprospecting. I investigate how molecular sequence, structure and physicochemical properties relate to the biological activity of peptides and natural products, with applications in drug discovery and biotechnology. I coordinate PepSpace and lead an interdisciplinary research group focused on biomolecular technologies.

I hold a PhD in Biotechnology, with an emphasis on bioprospecting, and an MSc in Genetics and Molecular Biology, with an emphasis on bioinformatics, from the Federal University of Pará (UFPA). At UFOPA, I teach bioinformatics and structural biology and contribute to graduate training in Biosciences and the BIONORTE Biodiversity and Biotechnology network.

🧬 PepSpace

Explore the PepSpace web server →

PepSpace brings together machine learning models and analysis tools for cell-penetrating peptides (CPPs), blood–brain barrier-penetrating peptides (B3PPs) and quorum-sensing peptides (QSPs).

Module

What researchers can do

Bioactivity prediction

Screen peptide candidates using BrainPepPass, ConvBoost-CPP and QuorumPep-Pred.

Sequence analysis

Compare sequences with reference peptide classes, inspect conserved patterns and examine local sequence similarity.

Structure analysis

Investigate peptide structural and physicochemical features from PDB input.

The platform focuses on natural and synthetic non-modified peptides. Supported inputs depend on the module and model; see the platform documentation.

📚 Selected publications

Peptides, artificial intelligence and data science

2026 · Peptide chemical space
Navigating in the chemical space of peptides: computational strategies and molecular features to unveil their functional and drug-like properties
de Oliveira et al. Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics, 28, 11519–11545.
A review connecting molecular representations, dimensionality reduction and similarity networks with peptide function and drug-like properties.

2025 · Peptide physicochemical properties
Beyond Molecular Weight: Peptide Characteristics Influencing the Sensitivity of Retention to Changes in Organic Solvent in Reversed-Phase Chromatography
Descamps et al. ACS Omega, 10(25), 27089–27097.
Combines experiments on 18 peptides with PLS, correlation analysis and predictive models to investigate chromatographic retention.

2025 · Bioactive peptides and biological mechanisms
Role of Peptides in Skeletal Muscle Wasting: A Scoping Review
Naumovski et al. Journal of Cachexia, Sarcopenia and Muscle, 16(6), e70109.
A synthesis of peptide-associated mechanisms and research gaps relevant to muscle wasting and future peptide discovery.

2024 · Deep learning for cell penetration
Investigating molecular descriptors in cell-penetrating peptides prediction with deep learning: Employing N, O, and hydrophobicity according to the Eisenberg scale
Seixas Feio et al. PLOS ONE, 19(6), e0305253.
Introduces ConvBoost-CPP, combining a convolutional neural network with XGBoost, and examines the role of molecular descriptors. The study reports 91.3% independent-test accuracy for CPP prediction.

2024 · Machine learning for blood–brain barrier penetration
BrainPepPass: A Framework Based on Supervised Dimensionality Reduction for Predicting Blood-Brain Barrier-Penetrating Peptides
de Oliveira et al. Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling, 64(7), 2368–2382. First published online in December 2023.
Combines supervised manifold dimensionality reduction with extreme gradient boosting for B3PP prediction, including chemically modified peptides in the published framework. Code repository.

2022 · Membrane-penetrating peptides
Biological Membrane-Penetrating Peptides: Computational Prediction and Applications
de Oliveira et al. Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology, 12, 838259.
Reviews computational prediction, molecular properties and applications of CPPs and B3PPs.

2021 · Sequence- and structure-based peptide prediction
Predicting cell-penetrating peptides using machine learning algorithms and navigating in their chemical space
de Oliveira et al. Scientific Reports, 11, 7628.
Introduces BChemRF-CPPred, integrating sequence and structure descriptors to explore CPP chemical space. The study reports 90.66% accuracy and AUC 0.9365 in the independent test using PDB input.

Performance values refer to the evaluation datasets and input formats used in each publication.

Natural products and computational bioprospecting

2022 · Natural-product virtual screening
Virtual screening of natural products against 5-enolpyruvylshikimate-3-phosphate synthase using the Anagreen herbicide-like natural compound library
de Oliveira et al. RSC Advances, 12, 18834–18847.
Combines a curated natural-product library with computational screening against a biologically relevant enzyme target.

2021 · Bioprospecting methodology
Applications of Virtual Screening in Bioprospecting: Facts, Shifts, and Perspectives to Explore the Chemo-Structural Diversity of Natural Products
Santana et al. Frontiers in Chemistry, 9, 662688.
