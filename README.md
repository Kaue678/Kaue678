## About me

I am an Associate Professor at **UFOPA**, Brazil, working at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, data science, structural bioinformatics and molecular bioprospecting**. My research investigates how molecular sequence, structure and physicochemical properties relate to the biological activity of peptides and natural products, with applications in drug discovery and biotechnology.

I coordinate **[PepSpace](https://pepspace.org/)** and lead an interdisciplinary research group focused on biomolecular technologies. My main interests include cell-penetrating peptides, blood–brain barrier-penetrating peptides, quorum-sensing peptides, chemical-space analysis and computational screening of bioactive compounds.

I hold a **PhD in Biotechnology**, with an emphasis on bioprospecting, and an **MSc in Genetics and Molecular Biology**, with an emphasis on bioinformatics, from the Federal University of Pará (UFPA). At UFOPA, I teach bioinformatics and structural biology and contribute to graduate training in Biosciences and the BIONORTE Biodiversity and Biotechnology network.

## PepSpace

### Exploring peptide sequence, structure and bioactivity

**[Open the PepSpace web server →](https://pepspace.org/)**

PepSpace provides a web interface for investigating peptides with biotechnological and biomedical applications. It brings together machine learning models and complementary analysis tools for **cell-penetrating peptides (CPPs)**, **blood–brain barrier-penetrating peptides (B3PPs)** and **quorum-sensing peptides (QSPs)**.

| Module | What researchers can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Bioactivity prediction** | Screen peptide candidates using models including BrainPepPass, ConvBoost-CPP and QuorumPep-Pred. |
| **Sequence analysis** | Compare sequences with reference peptide classes, inspect conserved patterns and examine local sequence similarity. |
| **Structure analysis** | Investigate peptide structural and physicochemical features from PDB input. |

The platform focuses on natural and synthetic **non-modified peptides**. Supported inputs depend on the module and model; see the [platform documentation](https://pepspace.org/about) for details.

### Research outputs

| Output | Contribution or published result | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Peptide chemical-space review · 2026** | Connects molecular representations, dimensionality reduction and similarity networks with peptide function and drug-like properties. | [PCCP](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5CP04611D) |
| **Peptide chromatography · 2025** | Combines experiments on **18 peptides** with PLS, correlation analysis and predictive models to investigate retention behavior. | [ACS Omega](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.5c02327) |
| **ConvBoost-CPP · 2024** | Combines a convolutional neural network with XGBoost; the study reports **91.3% independent-test accuracy** for CPP prediction. | [PLOS ONE](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0305253) |
| **BrainPepPass · 2024** | Combines supervised manifold dimensionality reduction with extreme gradient boosting for B3PP prediction, including chemically modified peptides in the published framework. | [JCIM](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.3c00951) |
| **BChemRF-CPPred · 2021** | Integrates sequence and structure descriptors; the study reports **90.66% accuracy and AUC 0.9365** in the independent test using PDB input. | [Scientific Reports](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-87134-w) |

Performance values refer to the evaluation datasets and input formats used in the respective publications. BrainPepPass was first published online in December 2023 and appeared in the journal's 2024 volume.

## Selected publications

### Peptides, artificial intelligence and data science

**2026 · Peptide chemical space**  
[**Navigating in the chemical space of peptides: computational strategies and molecular features to unveil their functional and drug-like properties**](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5CP04611D)  
de Oliveira et al. *Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics*, **28**, 11519–11545.  
A conceptual review linking peptide representations and computational analysis to molecular function and pharmaceutical properties.

**2025 · Peptide physicochemical properties**  
[**Beyond Molecular Weight: Peptide Characteristics Influencing the Sensitivity of Retention to Changes in Organic Solvent in Reversed-Phase Chromatography**](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.5c02327)

An experimental and data-driven investigation of molecular features associated with peptide chromatographic retention.

**2025 · Bioactive peptides and biological mechanisms**

Naumovski et al. *Journal of Cachexia, Sarcopenia and Muscle*, **16**(6), e70109.  
A synthesis of peptide-associated mechanisms and research gaps relevant to muscle wasting and future peptide discovery.

**2024 · Deep learning for cell penetration**  
[**Investigating molecular descriptors in cell-penetrating peptides prediction with deep learning: Employing N, O, and hydrophobicity according to the Eisenberg scale**](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0305253)  
Seixas Feio et al. *PLOS ONE*, **19**(6), e0305253.  
Introduces ConvBoost-CPP and examines how molecular descriptors influence CPP prediction.

**2024 · Machine learning for blood–brain barrier penetration**

de Oliveira et al. *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling*, **64**(7), 2368–2382. First published online in 2023.  
A predictive framework connecting supervised dimensionality reduction with peptide classification. [Code repository](https://github.com/ewerton-cristhian/BrainPepPass).

**2022 · Membrane-penetrating peptides**  
[**Biological Membrane-Penetrating Peptides: Computational Prediction and Applications**](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcimb.2022.838259)  
de Oliveira et al. *Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology*, **12**, 838259.  
Reviews computational prediction, molecular properties and applications of CPPs and B3PPs.

**2021 · Sequence- and structure-based peptide prediction**  
[**Predicting cell-penetrating peptides using machine learning algorithms and navigating in their chemical space**](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-87134-w)  
de Oliveira et al. *Scientific Reports*, **11**, 7628.  
Introduces BChemRF-CPPred and investigates the molecular space associated with cell-penetrating peptides.

## Methods and research tools

- **Machine learning:** neural networks, support vector machines, Gaussian process classification, gradient boosting and convolutional neural networks.
- **Chemical data analysis:** sequence- and structure-derived descriptors, feature selection, correlation analysis, PCA, PLS and supervised manifold learning.
- **Molecular modeling:** protein structural analysis, molecular docking, molecular dynamics and investigation of molecular interactions.
- **Scientific computing:** Python and R for molecular data processing, statistical analysis and predictive modeling.

My collaborative software outputs include **BChemRF-CPPred, BrainPepPass and ConvBoost-CPP**, registered as computer programs with Brazil's National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI).
